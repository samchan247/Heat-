Common Mistakes

常犯錯誤

  • Thermometers: Liquid-in-glass cannot measure solid blocks due to poor thermal contact. Use an IR thermometer.
  • Latent Heat Control: Forgetting the control setup for ice melted by room temperature causes an underestimation of $l_f$.
  • Kinetic Theory: Heavy and light gas molecules at the same temperature have the same KE, but heavy ones move slower.
  • 溫度計： 玻璃液體溫度計不能量度固體，因為熱接觸不良。應使用紅外線溫度計。
  • 潛熱對照： 忘記扣除室溫熔化冰的對照裝置，會導致低估 $l_f$。
  • 分子運動論： 相同溫度下，重分子和輕分子動能相同，但重分子移動較慢。

Q1: Phase Change & Mixtures

Q1：物態變化與混合

0.50 kg of ice at 0°C is mixed with 1.2 kg of water at 60°C in an insulated container. (c_water = 4200 J kg-1 °C-1, l_fusion = 3.34 × 105 J kg-1)

(a) Show by calculation that all ice will melt. (2 marks)

(b) Calculate the final equilibrium temperature. (2 marks)

將 0.50 kg，0°C 的冰與 1.2 kg，60°C 的水在隔熱容器內混合。(c_水 = 4200 J kg-1 °C-1, l_熔化 = 3.34 × 105 J kg-1)

(a) 藉計算證明所有冰均會熔化。 (2分)

(b) 計算最後的平衡溫度。 (2分)

Hint: (a) Compare energy to melt all ice ($ml$) vs max energy hot water can give ($mc\Delta T$ dropping to 0). (b) Let final temp = T. Heat lost by hot water = Heat gained melting ice + Heat gained by melted ice warming up.

提示: (a) 比較熔化冰所需能量 ($ml$) 與熱水能釋放的最高能量 ($mc\Delta T$ 降至0)。(b) 設最後溫度 = T。熱水失去的熱 = 熔化冰吸收的熱 + 冰水升溫吸收的熱。

(a) Energy to melt ice = 0.50 × 3.34 × 105 = 167,000 J [1M]
Max energy from water = 1.2 × 4200 × 60 = 302,400 J.
Since 302,400 > 167,000, all ice melts. [1A]

(b) 1.2 × 4200 × (60 - T) = 167000 + 0.50 × 4200 × (T - 0) [1M]
302400 - 5040T = 167000 + 2100T → T = 19.0 °C [1A]

(a) 熔化冰所需能量 = 0.50 × 3.34 × 105 = 167,000 J [1M]
水可釋放最高能量 = 1.2 × 4200 × 60 = 302,400 J。
因為 302,400 > 167,000，所有冰會熔化。[1A]

(b) 1.2 × 4200 × (60 - T) = 167000 + 0.50 × 4200 × (T - 0) [1M]
302400 - 5040T = 167000 + 2100T → T = 19.0 °C [1A]

Q2: Power, Time, and Phase Changes

Q2：功率、時間與物態變化

0.8 kg of a solid substance X is heated steadily by a 100 W electric heater. The temperature-time graph of the substance is shown below. Assume no heat is lost to the surroundings.

使用 100 W 的電熱器穩定加熱 0.8 kg 固體物質 X。其溫度-時間圖像如下。假設無熱量流失。

(a) Calculate the specific heat capacity of substance X in its solid state. (2 marks)

(b) Calculate the specific latent heat of fusion of substance X. (2 marks)

(c) Deduce whether the specific heat capacity in liquid state is greater or smaller than in solid state. (2 marks)

(a) 計算物質 X 在固態時的比熱容量。 (2分)

(b) 計算物質 X 的熔化比潛熱。 (2分)

(c) 推斷物質 X 在液態時的比熱容量是較固態時大還是小？ (2分)

Hint: (a) Convert minutes to seconds! E = Pt = mcΔT. (c) A gentler slope means the temperature rises more slowly for the same energy.

提示: (a) 將分鐘轉換為秒！E = Pt = mcΔT。(c) 斜率較平緩代表在相同能量下溫度上升較慢。

(a) Heating time = 4 mins = 240 s. ΔT = 80 - 20 = 60 °C.
Pt = mcΔT → 100 × 240 = 0.8 × c × 60 [1M]
c = 500 J kg-1 °C-1 [1A]

(b) Melting time = 10 - 4 = 6 mins = 360 s.
Pt = ml_f → 100 × 360 = 0.8 × l_f [1M]
l_f = 45,000 J kg-1 [1A]

(c) It is greater. [1A]
The slope of the liquid phase is less steep. A gentler slope means slower temperature rise for the same power input, indicating larger heat capacity. [1A]

(a) 加熱時間 = 4 分鐘 = 240 秒。ΔT = 80 - 20 = 60 °C。
Pt = mcΔT → 100 × 240 = 0.8 × c × 60 [1M]
c = 500 J kg-1 °C-1 [1A]

(b) 熔化時間 = 10 - 4 = 6 分鐘 = 360 秒。
Pt = ml_f → 100 × 360 = 0.8 × l_f [1M]
l_f = 45,000 J kg-1 [1A]

(c) 較大。[1A]
液態階段的斜率較平緩。代表在相同功率輸入下溫度上升較慢，這表示它有較大的熱容量。[1A]

Q3: Cooling Curves

Q3：冷卻曲線

0.3 kg of a hot beverage (c = 4000 J/kg°C) at 85°C is placed in a 25°C room. After 5 minutes, its temperature drops to 65°C.

(a) Calculate the average rate of heat loss (in Watts) during these 5 minutes. (2 marks)

(b) Predict whether the temperature drop in the next 5 minutes will be greater than, equal to, or less than 20°C. Explain briefly. (2 marks)

0.3 kg 熱飲 (c = 4000 J/kg°C) 初始溫度為 85°C，放於 25°C 的房間。5 分鐘後降至 65°C。

(a) 計算在這 5 分鐘內，平均散熱速率 (以瓦特 Watts 為單位)。 (2分)

(b) 預測在接下來的 5 分鐘內，溫度下降將大於、等於還是小於 20°C？試簡略解釋。 (2分)

Hint: Rate of heat loss = Energy / time(s). Rate of cooling depends on the temperature difference between object and surroundings.

提示: 散熱速率 = 能量 / 時間(秒)。冷卻速率取決於物體與四周環境的溫度差。

(a) Total heat lost Q = mcΔT = 0.3 × 4000 × 20 = 24000 J [1M]
Average rate = Q / t = 24000 / (5 × 60) = 80 W [1A]

(b) Less than 20°C. [1A]
Rate of heat loss decreases as the temperature difference between the beverage and the room decreases. [1A]

(a) 總散失熱量 Q = mcΔT = 0.3 × 4000 × 20 = 24000 J [1M]
平均速率 = Q / t = 24000 / (5 × 60) = 80 W [1A]

(b) 小於 20°C。[1A]
散熱速率會隨著熱飲與房間之間的溫度差縮小而減慢。[1A]
